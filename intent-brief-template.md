# Intent Brief Template

## Title

Short name of the unit of work.


## Business Intent

Why this work matters.


## User Intent

What the user should be able to do after this work.


## Functional Intent

What the system must do.


## Non-Functional Intent

How the system must behave.


## Scope

Included work.


## Out Of Scope

Work intentionally not included.


## Assumptions

Assumptions Codex should preserve or verify.


## Risks

Known risks or unclear areas.


## Acceptance Criteria

Testable completion criteria.


## Test Expectations

Unit, UI, eval, or manual checks expected.


## Demo Expectation

What should be shown at the end.


## Human Decisions Required

Questions or approvals needed from the user.
